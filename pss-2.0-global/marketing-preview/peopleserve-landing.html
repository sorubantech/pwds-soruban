The problems you already know about

Not features — the things that cost nonprofit teams the most time, and what changes when the work sits in one system.

Receipts are issued by hand, late, and sometimes twice

Receipts are numbered by your own sequence, issued the moment a donation is recorded, and acknowledged by email automatically — with a full audit trail behind every number.

Grant spend lives in a spreadsheet nobody trusts at reporting time

Every grant carries its own budget, tranches and expenses. Spend is checked against the remaining balance as it is booked, so the funder report is a query, not a reconstruction.

The same donor exists four times under four spellings

One contact record per person, with duplicate detection on entry and a merge path when duplicates slip through. Giving history, communications and consent stay attached to the person.

Nobody can say what a restricted donation was actually spent on

Money is allocated to a purpose when it arrives and drawn down against it when it is used. A restricted gift can be traced from the donor, through the programme, to the beneficiary payment it funded.

Every campaign page needs a developer and two weeks

Donation pages, crowdfunding pages, peer-to-peer campaigns, event registration and volunteer sign-up are built and published from inside the platform. Gifts land on the same ledger as everything else, already attributed to the campaign.

The annual report is assembled by hand from six exports

Dashboards and reports read the live records — donations, cases, grants, events, volunteer hours — instead of a spreadsheet copy. The number on the board pack is the number in the system.

In detail

What that actually means day to day

Follow the money: it comes in, it is directed somewhere, it is spent on someone, and the whole thing has to be administered. Everything below is in the product today.

Getting money in

Every route a gift can arrive by, landing on one ledger.

  • Donation capture for cash, cheque, bank transfer, card and online payment, with your own receipt numbering per book
  • Recurring giving and pledges, with what was promised tracked separately from what has been received
  • Gifts in kind valued and recorded alongside cash, so the total raised is the real total
  • Matching gifts, so a corporate match is attributed to both the donor and the matching organisation
  • Branded donation, crowdfunding and peer-to-peer pages built in the platform and published on your own domain
  • Card payments through your own gateway account — Razorpay, PayU and Braintree — so settlement goes to your bank, not ours
  • Field collection for cash raised away from the office, reconciled back against the collector
  • Automatic acknowledgement email with the receipt attached, the moment the gift is recorded

Directing it

Restricted money that stays restricted, and can prove it did.

  • Donation purposes, categories and groups, so a gift is directed the moment it is taken
  • Restricted and unrestricted funds with live allocated, collected and remaining balances
  • Grants with documents, budgets and tranches, and receipts recorded against a named bank account
  • Programmes carrying their own funding model — individual sponsorship, pooled, grant-funded or mixed
  • Funding sources attached to a programme after approval, with each source's contribution tracked separately
  • Payment logs per funding source, so pledged, transferred and spent are three different numbers
  • Spend checked against the remaining balance as it is booked, not discovered at year end

Reaching people

Beneficiaries, supporters and everyone in between, on one register.

  • Beneficiary intake with verification before a case can be opened, and its own identifier sequence
  • Case files with assessments, service logs, milestones and outcomes, reviewed through an approval workflow
  • Assistance paid out against a case and reconciled to the funding source that covered it
  • Events with public registration, paid or free ticketing, ticket templates and check-in
  • Volunteers with public sign-up, skills, shift scheduling and logged hours
  • Membership types, subscriptions and renewals for organisations that run a membership base
  • Families and organisations modelled as first-class records, not a text field on a person

Running it

The administration that decides whether a system survives its first year.

  • Roles and permissions granted per screen and per action, so finance, caseworkers and fundraisers see different systems
  • Your branding, logo and colours across the app and every public page and email
  • Multi-currency with explicit exchange rates, and English, Bengali and Arabic including right-to-left layout
  • Your own numbering sequences for receipts, cases, beneficiaries and more — set once, then enforced
  • Bulk import from spreadsheets with validation and duplicate checking before anything is written
  • Configurable grids, saved filters and per-user column layouts on every list in the product
  • Dashboards and widgets assembled from live data, plus reports and exports for the board and the auditor
  • An audit trail on financial and beneficiary records — who changed what, and when

Not every organisation needs every one of these. Capability areas are switched on per organisation and permissions are granted per role, so your team sees the part of the platform they actually work in — and you can turn more on later without a migration.

Trust

Security and your data

You are being asked to put donor and beneficiary records into someone else's system. Here is exactly what that means.

Your data is yours

Each organisation's data is isolated from every other organisation on the platform. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to train anything.

Access is controlled by role

Permissions are granted per role, per screen and per action — view, create, edit, approve — so caseworkers, finance staff and fundraisers each see only what their job requires.

We never hold card details

Card payments go straight to your own gateway account. Card numbers never touch our systems, and settlement goes to your bank, not through ours.

Changes are recorded

Financial and beneficiary records carry an audit trail — who changed what, and when — for internal review and external audit.

Encrypted and backed up

Data is encrypted in transit, held in managed cloud infrastructure, and backed up on a regular schedule.

No lock-in

Your records can be exported in standard formats whenever you ask. Leaving is a process, not a negotiation.

Privacy

Consent is captured against the contact record and honoured by the communications module. Our privacy policy sets out how we handle personal data.

Sign-in is host-gated

Your users sign in on your own workspace address, and our own staff cannot sign in there at all. Which door an account may use is enforced by the server, not chosen by the browser.

Rules are enforced on the server

Permission checks, approval steps and validation run in the API, not only in the page you can see. Bypassing the interface does not bypass the rule.

FAQ

Questions we are asked

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan covers contacts, donation collection and receipting, and it is a real plan rather than a trial that expires. You can move to a larger plan when your organisation needs the additional modules.

Can I sign up online right now?

No — and that is deliberate. Every workspace is configured for the organisation that will use it: your modules, roles, numbering, currencies and imported data. Send an enquiry and we will set it up with you.

How long does it take to go live?

For a small organisation with clean data, typically a couple of weeks. Larger migrations with historical giving records, open cases or active grants take longer, and we will give you a realistic timeline after the first conversation.

Can you import our existing donor data?

Yes. Contacts, giving history and beneficiary records can be imported from spreadsheets or from your current system. Data is checked for duplicates before it lands.

Do you support multiple currencies and languages?

Yes. The platform handles multiple currencies with explicit exchange rates, and the interface is available in English, Bengali and Arabic, including right-to-left layout.

Who can see our data?

Only the users you create, and only the parts their role permits. Your organisation's data is isolated from every other organisation on the platform.

What does support look like?

Every plan includes support; larger plans include faster response commitments and a named contact. Support covers the platform itself as well as questions about configuring it for a new programme.

Can we get our data out if we leave?

Yes, in standard exportable formats, on request. There is no exit fee and no hostage-taking of your records.

Which payment gateways do you support?

Razorpay, PayU and Braintree today, connected to your own merchant account. Donations settle into your bank, not into ours, and we never see or store card numbers. If you already have a gateway relationship, tell us which one and we will confirm before you commit to anything.

Do we get our own web address for donation pages?

Yes. Your workspace runs on your own address, and the donation, crowdfunding, peer-to-peer, event and volunteer pages you publish are served from it, carrying your logo and colours rather than ours.

Can we track what a restricted donation was actually spent on?

That is one of the main reasons the platform exists. A gift is directed to a purpose or fund when it is recorded; programmes draw down against their funding sources; assistance paid on a case is booked back to the source that covered it. The trail from donor to beneficiary is a query, not a reconstruction.

Does it handle offline donations, or only online?

Both, on the same ledger. Cash, cheque, bank transfer and field collection are recorded with the same receipting, purposes and reporting as online gifts — the report does not care which way the money arrived.

Can we run it alongside our accounting system?

Yes. The platform is your fundraising and programme record, not your general ledger, and its reports and exports are built to be handed to finance. Talk to us about your accounting package and we will be specific about what that handover looks like for you.

What if we only need part of it?

That is the normal case. Capability areas are switched on per organisation and permissions are granted per role, so a team that only does donations and events never sees case management. You can turn more on later without a migration.
